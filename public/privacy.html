Privacy policy

How we handle your information.

Last updated September 26, 2026

Who runs this site

Symbol & Spark LLC runs this website. KeepsiPuff is a brand and registered trade name of Symbol & Spark LLC.

Information you choose to send

The free coloring-sheet signup collects your email address and, if you provide it, your first name. Flodesk handles the signup and email delivery.

Technical information

Vercel hosts this site and processes basic technical information needed to serve and protect it, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and request logs.

We do not use analytics, advertising trackers, or tracking pixels on this website.

Email activity

Flodesk may record email opens and link clicks. We use that information to understand whether our emails are useful and working as expected.

You can unsubscribe from any KeepsiPuff email by using the unsubscribe link in the message.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the services that run the website and email signup, including Vercel and Flodesk. Those services process information under their own privacy terms.

Links to other sites

This site links to Amazon, Etsy, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, and other outside services. Their privacy policies govern what happens after you leave this site.

How long we keep information

We keep your email address and optional first name while you remain subscribed. Unsubscribing stops future marketing emails, subject to the records Flodesk keeps to honor your choice.

Your choices and requests

You may ask to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. Reply to any KeepsiPuff email you have received, and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

This site is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
